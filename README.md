# Geomatics Engineering Portfolio

A collection of practical geomatics and engineering projects demonstrating surveying workflows, geospatial data processing, mapping, and technical analysis.

## Areas of Focus

- **Land and Engineering Surveying** — Survey workflows, field data management, and engineering survey applications.
- **GNSS and Survey Data Processing** — Coordinate data handling, quality checks, processing workflows, and survey outputs.
- **Engineering Site Monitoring** — Analysis and visualisation of repeated survey observations to assess positional changes.
- **Topographic Surveying and Mapping** — Terrain representation, elevation data, contour mapping, and technical mapping outputs.
- **Geospatial Data Management** — Organising, validating, processing, and presenting spatial data.
